Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Quotes With Vibe (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android application. Quotes With Vibe is a quotes browsing app. You do not need to create an account to use it.

By using the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not require you to provide a name, email address, or password to use the app. We do not operate a user account system for Quotes With Vibe.

  • We do not ask for your identity details to sign in
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location for app features
  • Favorites, theme, and similar preferences are stored on your device

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect or process device and usage-related data in order to provide advertising, notifications, and content. Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We show ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads). AdMob and its partners may collect or use information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (for example device type, OS version, app version)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Ad interaction and performance data (views, clicks, and related metrics)

This information is used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and (where permitted) personalize ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data in ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Push Notifications (OneSignal)

We use OneSignal to send push notifications (for example app updates or content-related messages). If you allow notification permission, OneSignal may process data such as:

  • Push notification tokens / device push identifiers
  • Device information needed to deliver notifications
  • Notification delivery and interaction events

You can disable notifications in your device or app settings. OneSignal Privacy Policy: https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy
